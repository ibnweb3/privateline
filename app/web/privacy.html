What this does not hide

  • Your texts. SMS is not encrypted. Your mobile network, and the SMS gateway, can read your messages, just as they can read bank SMS alerts. Replies never include your PIN, and a trade needs your PIN plus 2 of 3 operators.
  • From the operators. The vault's three operators can see every account, by account ID, because they check limits and prices. They never see phone numbers.
  • Feedback. If you fill in the feedback form, your answers are stored without your name, email or phone number. We keep nothing that identifies who gave them.
  • Canton Coin. Canton Coin transfers are public on explorers. PrivateLine's trades have no Canton Coin leg on the ledger; on MainNet the desk's own Cantex swaps would.
