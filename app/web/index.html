How it works

Text a command

BAL, PRICE GOLD, BUY SPY 20, SELL GOLD ALL. Amounts are in US dollars, and fractions of a share are fine.

Confirm with your PIN

Every trade is restated first. Reply YES and your PIN within 2 minutes. Three wrong PINs pause trading.

2 of 3 operators approve

The SMS operator proposes the trade on the ledger. It runs only when an independent checker confirms the price and your limits, through BitSafe's Decentralization Manager.

The vault's three operators

Your money sits in a vault that no single operator controls. Any 2 of the 3 can approve; one alone can't.

  • SMS operator (node 1)
    Reads your texts and proposes every action.
  • Price checker (node 2) checking
    Refuses trades at off-market prices, on its own price data.
  • Risk checker (node 3) checking
    Checks limits and holds phone changes for 24 hours against SIM swaps.

Who can see your money

YouYour balances and trades, on the ledger, under your own Canton party.
Other usersNothing. Not your account, not your trades, not that you exist.
The deskThe size of each trade, never who made it. It never sees an account.
The 3 operatorsYour account by its ID, to check limits. Never your phone number: the ledger only holds a keyed tag of it.
The publicNothing. These contracts are not on any public explorer.

Text messages themselves are not encrypted: your mobile network can read them, just as it can read bank SMS alerts. What PrivateLine keeps private is the ledger. See it live for your account.

PrivateLine runs on BitSafe's Decentralization Manager LocalNet for HackCanton Season 3. Balances are demo dollars and demo mirrors of tokens that trade on Canton MainNet (SPYe, QQQe, eXAU, eXAG, cBTC, cETH); prices are live from Cantex. No real money is involved.
